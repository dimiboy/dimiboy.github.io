Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

KeepLocate ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the KeepLocate mobile application on Android.

Core Privacy Principle: KeepLocate operates under an offline-first, local-storage architecture. All your notes, checklists, drawings, voice recordings, and location coordinates reside locally on your device unless you explicitly opt in to Google Tasks sync or Google Drive cloud backup.

1. Information We Collect and Process

1.1 User Content

When you create notes, checklists, audio memos, drawings, or take photos within KeepLocate, this data is saved directly in a private SQLite Room database stored in your device's internal storage. We do not operate external intermediate servers, and we never collect, view, or sell your notes or media.

1.2 Location Information

To enable location-based reminders (geofencing), KeepLocate requests the following permissions:

  • Approximate and Precise Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Needed to pinpoint reminder locations chosen by you on the interactive map.
  • Background Location (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION): Needed so that Android OS can trigger arrival or departure alerts when your device enters or exits a geofenced area, even when the application is closed or in the background.

Important: Your live location is evaluated exclusively on-device via official Google Play Services Geofencing APIs. KeepLocate does not record location history, does not track your movements, and never transmits your coordinates to any external server.

2. Google User Data and Google API Services Policy

KeepLocate provides optional integrations with Google services. If you choose to connect your Google account:

2.1 Scopes Requested

  • Google Tasks API (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks): Used solely to read, create, update, and mark tasks as completed between KeepLocate checklists and your Google Tasks lists.
  • Google Drive Application Data Folder (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata): Used solely to save and restore your encrypted application backup archives in your hidden, app-specific Google Drive storage folder. KeepLocate cannot access any other files in your Google Drive.

2.2 Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Limited Use Disclosure:
KeepLocate's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We do not share your Google user data with any third parties or advertising networks.
  • We do not use your Google user data for advertising, market research, or profiling.
  • We do not use your Google user data to train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • Data retrieved from Google APIs is processed directly between your device and Google's official endpoints using secure TLS/HTTPS encryption. No intermediate servers or proxies are involved.

3. Data Protection Mechanisms for Sensitive Data

We take the security of your sensitive user data very seriously and implement industry-standard safeguards to protect it:

  • Security procedures are in place to protect the confidentiality of your data: We implement administrative, physical, and technical measures designed to safeguard your personal data and Google user data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
  • We use encryption to protect your information: All communications between KeepLocate and Google APIs (Google Tasks API and Google Drive) are transmitted exclusively over secure, encrypted network connections using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3 with HTTPS). Data in transit cannot be intercepted or read by unauthorized third parties.
  • Protection of Data at Rest: Sensitive data stored on your device, including authentication tokens and cached note information, resides within Android's protected application sandbox. OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely in protected storage and are never exposed to other applications, transmitted to external servers, or stored in plaintext.
  • Principle of Least Privilege: KeepLocate only requests the minimal sensitive scopes strictly required for task synchronization and private backups. We never request full Google Drive access, Gmail access, or broad profile access.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We believe in transparent data stewardship and provide users with complete control over their information:

  • Retention Policy: We will retain your personal information for the length of time needed to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. Because KeepLocate does not operate external intermediary servers, your data is retained locally on your device only for as long as you maintain the application.
  • Expiration and Destruction: When the data retention period expires for a given type of data or when you choose to remove the application, we will delete or destroy it permanently from the device.
  • User-Initiated Deletion: You may request for your data to be deleted at any time:
    • You can disconnect your Google Account inside KeepLocate (Settings → Disconnect), which immediately purges all stored OAuth credentials and active synchronization tokens from your device.
    • You can revoke KeepLocate's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account Permissions Manager.
    • You can delete all locally stored notes, attachments, and reminders by clearing application data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
    • You can also contact the developer directly at dimiboy@gmail.com to request assistance with data removal.

5. Device Permissions Overview

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required on Android 13+ to post reminder alerts and pinned notifications.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to trigger user-scheduled time reminders at exact minutes.
  • Biometric Sensor (USE_BIOMETRIC): Handled strictly via Android BiometricPrompt to protect locked notes locally. No biometric credentials ever leave Android's secure hardware enclave.

6. Children's Privacy

KeepLocate does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
